Carey: The Get Well Bot

About Carey

Hello, I'm Carey!!!




In todays constantly growing and evolving world, the healthcare institution has improved significantly over the course of many years. However, despite development in medical fields due to technological advancement, for many civilians, most health-related problems go undiagnosed and untreated. This is due to several reasons. One of the leading causes is due to ignorance and lack of awareness about ones own symptoms.

It is due to lack of proper knowledge about where and how they should report certain symptoms if identified. In addition to these, most health-related issues couldve been easily avoided, if proper prevention had been adapted into ones lifestyle, but due to limited knowledge, it became unavoidable.

In todays stress-filled and anxiety inducing world, timely and accurate healthcare guidance significantly impacts our well-being. To tackle all these problems, we introduce our minor project, Carey: The Get Well Bot -- a Healthcare Chatbot that is an innovative solution to provide free, accessible, accurate, reliable and personalized chatbot willing to assist the user whenever they need.

Carey: The Get Well Bot is made with fusion of modern technology and immense care for its users. It utilizes the power of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) to create a conversational interface that allows users to interact with the healthcare system effortlessly. Whether one has questions about symptoms, needs medication reminders, require general health advice or simply seeks emotional support for mental well-being, our chatbot Carey: The Get Well Bot is here to assist you.
